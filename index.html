What gets an alert

Every ~90 seconds while a run is active, GemRadar reads new and trending pools on every chain GeckoTerminal indexes, plus DexScreener's new profiles, boosts and community takeovers.

A coin only reaches you when it:

  1. passes the market filter (enough liquidity, trades and volume, not dumping),
  2. passes every scam check: honeypot/tax, mint & freeze authority, dev wallet, top-10 holders, single whales, linked insider wallets (bubblemap-style clusters), bundles and same-size wallet clusters, liquidity lock,
  3. passes every staying-power check: at least 6 hours old, watched by GemRadar for 3+ hours, no more than 45% below its peak, trading still active, liquidity, market cap and holders not falling over 3 hours, and holders not growing faster than real buys (bot/airdrop wallets),
  4. and scores at least –/100.

Coins younger than 6 hours normally appear only in Live: most pump.fun launches die within the first hours.

🔥 FOMO fast lane: a young coin can still alert early when 3+ traders on the FOMO app bought it within 2 hours (or it's top 20 on FOMO trending), FOMO traders aren't selling it, all scam checks pass, it hasn't crashed from its peak and its holders aren't bots. These alerts are riskier and are marked 🔥.

🐋 Smart money: alerts before the move. FOMO-app traders (3+, or 2+ from FOMO's top-150 leaderboard) already hold the coin and haven't sold, while the price hasn't run yet (under +100% in 6h). It still has to pass every scam check and not be crashing or losing liquidity. Quiet coins held by FOMO traders skip the minimum-trading filter, because accumulation is quiet by nature. Some of these will never run.

🧩 Early project: a real project (website, own X account, ideally made before the coin, good GeckoTerminal score, CoinGecko listing, tech theme or a new chain like Robinhood) with at least one FOMO holder and no more FOMO sellers than buyers, under $1.5M, forming a base: at least +15% above its 24-hour low and not dumping, but not spiking yet (under +60% in 1h, +100% in 6h). An early launch pump-and-crash doesn't block it. Max 3 a day.

🚀 Early launch: a 1-6 hour old coin that is clearly a real project (score 6+/14) with clean wallets (top 10 ≤25%, dev ≤3%, bundles/clusters ≤8%), deep liquidity ($40k+ and 5%+ of market cap), organic demand (100+ buyers an hour, 1-3x more buys than sells, no bot holders), holding its gains for 30+ minutes (max 30% below its peak, max +150% in 1h), under $5M. Max 3 a day. Higher risk: young coins move fast both ways.

🌱 Fresh launch: the really early ones, often under $10k: 15 minutes to 24 hours old, $3k-$150k market cap (bonding-curve coins on pump.fun and similar count). Only when a real founder/team is behind it: the coin's own X account (not a tweet, community or borrowed big account), ideally made at least a week before the coin, plus followers, a real website, Telegram, a description, a tech theme, a paid DexScreener profile or FOMO traders buying (6+/12). It must pass every scam check (incl. serial-rugger devs), with 40+ holders, clean wallets (top 10 ≤30%, biggest wallet ≤8%, dev ≤5%, bundles/clusters ≤12%), real two-sided buying (25+ buys an hour) and be holding up (watched 10+ minutes, max 40% below its peak). Max 4 a day, 1 per scan. Lottery tickets: most launches still die.

💎 Gem and 🏆 Survivor no longer alert on a spike: not more than +100% in 1h or +200% in 6h.

Maximum – alerts per day; only very high scores break that limit. Coins on chains that can't be safety-checked are shown in Live but never alerted.

Score parts

Staying power 25 how long it survived, distance from its peak, higher lows over 6h, holder and liquidity growth over 3h, steady trading.
Traction 20 liquidity, volume vs market cap, trades, buy/sell balance, unique buyers.
FOMO app 15 how many FOMO traders bought (6h), FOMO trending rank, thesis posts; minus points when FOMO traders sell.
Holders 13 holder count, holders gained per hour, spread.
Hype 12 X/website/Telegram, X followers, DexScreener boosts & takeovers, trending lists, 4chan /biz/.
Momentum 10 1h & 6h price trend plus the buzz meter (volume speeding up, more buyers than sellers, keeps showing up in trending, new holders keep coming).
Upside 5 smaller market cap = more room to grow.

Limits

X's own search API isn't free, so X hype = the coin's account followers plus a button to open live X posts. Bundle data for Solana comes from a free service that is sometimes offline; the same-size-wallet check always runs. GitHub can start runs late, so alerts come within minutes, not seconds.

Not financial advice. Most new tokens go to zero; passing the checks lowers the scam risk, it does not remove it.
